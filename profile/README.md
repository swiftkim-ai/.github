## What lives here

This is the home for my larger projects. Most of the repositories are private, so there may not be much to browse.

The work falls into three groups:

- **Tools for small businesses**: point of sale, print-shop job tracking, board and meeting records.
- **Learning tools that work offline**: desktop apps for classrooms where the internet cannot be relied on.
- **Voice and AI helpers**: assistants that answer a phone call or do routine desk work.

## How things are built

- Small, plain, and tested. Boring technology first.
- Every project keeps a written record of what was decided and why.
- Nothing ships without running it.
